# About Page Quality Checklist

## Contents
- Answers the 3 questions
- Belongs
- Does not belong

**Purpose:** Pass/fail gate for any about page draft before it is shown or published.

## Answers the 3 questions
- [ ] What the community is, in the first two lines
- [ ] Who it is for, specifically
- [ ] Why join, with deliverables and an outcome

## Belongs
- [ ] Short punchy bullets, no paragraph longer than 3 lines
- [ ] Deliverables that really exist (classroom, calls, community)
- [ ] At least one real success story or testimonial (or a clearly marked slot)

## Does not belong
- [ ] No biography section
- [ ] No external links
- [ ] None of these phrases: "no fluff, just results", "remove the guesswork", "stop overthinking", "you don't need more X, you need what works", or similar AI filler
- [ ] No em dashes (the owner's voice (profile Voice section) rule)
