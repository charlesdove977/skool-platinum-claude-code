## Launch Sequence Email Template:

### Email 1 — Pre-Launch

**Subject:** I’ve been building something for [AUDIENCE]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Over the past [TIME PERIOD], I’ve heard versions of the same question:

“[QUESTION YOUR AUDIENCE KEEPS ASKING]?”

So I’ve been building something specifically for people who want to [DESIRED RESULT].

It’s called [COMMUNITY NAME], and it opens on [DATE].

Inside, you’ll have one place to get [BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF TRAINING], [SUPPORT], and [COMMUNITY/ACCOUNTABILITY].

I’ll send you the full details when it opens.

Because you’re already on this list, you’ll also receive the founding-member offer before I share it anywhere else.

— [NAME]



### Email 2 — Doors open

**Subject:** [COMMUNITY NAME] is officially open

Hi [FIRST NAME],

For the past [TIME PERIOD], I’ve been helping [AUDIENCE] with [PROBLEM OR SUBJECT].

One question keeps coming up:

“[REAL QUESTION IN THEIR WORDS]?”

That is why I built [COMMUNITY NAME].

It gives you one place to [PRIMARY OUTCOME] with:

- [SPECIFIC DELIVERABLE]
- [SPECIFIC DELIVERABLE]
- [SPECIFIC DELIVERABLE]
- [ACCESS, FEEDBACK OR ACCOUNTABILITY]

The founding-member rate is [PRICE].

That rate is available until [DATE AND TIME] / for the first [NUMBER] members. After that, the price becomes [FUTURE PRICE].

I’m limiting the founding group because [REAL CAPACITY REASON: PERSONAL FEEDBACK, SMALL CALLS, ONBOARDING, ETC.].

— [NAME]

P.S. Our first [CALL, CHALLENGE OR EVENT] happens on [DATE]. Join before then and you can participate from the beginning.



### Email 3 — Show what happens after joining

**Subject:** What your first week inside ___ looks like

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Here is exactly what happens when you join [COMMUNITY NAME].

First: Complete [SHORT START-HERE LESSON OR ASSESSMENT].

This helps you [IMMEDIATE RESULT].

Next: Post your [INTRODUCTION, AUDIT, PLAN OR QUESTION].

I’ll [REVIEW IT, RESPOND, PROVIDE FEEDBACK OR CONNECT THEM TO A RESOURCE].

Then: Join us for [WEEKLY CALL OR EVENT], where we [SPECIFIC PURPOSE].

By the end of your first week, you should have:

- [SMALL WIN]
- [SMALL WIN]
- [CLEAR NEXT STEP]

Here is a look at the classroom:

[INSERT ONE CLEAN SCREENSHOT]

You can see everything included here:

[IMAGE OF THE COMMUNITY]

The founding rate remains available until [DEADLINE].

— [NAME]



### Email 4 — Questions and objections

**Subject:** A few questions about [COMMUNITY NAME]

Hi [FIRST NAME],

I’ve received a few questions since opening [COMMUNITY NAME].

“Is this for beginners?”

[DIRECT ANSWER. EXPLAIN THE STARTING POINT.]

“How much time will this require?”

[REALISTIC WEEKLY TIME REQUIREMENT.]

“How is this different from [COURSE, FREE CONTENT OR OTHER OPTION]?”

[EXPLAIN THE SPECIFIC DIFFERENCE WITHOUT ATTACKING THE ALTERNATIVE.]

“Can I cancel?”

[EXPLAIN THE ACTUAL TERMS.]

“What should I do first?”

[EXPLAIN THE FIRST ACTION AND FIRST SMALL WIN.]

You can review everything included here:

[IMAGE OF THE COMMUNITY]

The founding rate ends [DATE AND TIME].

— [NAME]



### Email 5 — Final day

**Subject:** Last call — founding access closes tonight

Hi [FIRST NAME],

The founding offer for [COMMUNITY NAME] closes tonight at [TIME AND TIME ZONE].

You’ll receive:

- [CORE DELIVERABLE]
- [CORE DELIVERABLE]
- [CORE DELIVERABLE]
- [SUPPORT OR ACCESS]

The founding rate is [PRICE]. After tonight, it becomes [FUTURE PRICE] / the founding bonus is removed.

[JOIN BEFORE THE OFFER CLOSES]

This is the final launch email I’ll send.

If the community is right for you, join before [TIME]. If it is not, you will continue receiving [NORMAL NEWSLETTER OR CONTENT] as usual.

— [NAME]

P.S. The deadline is [TIME AND TIME ZONE]. Here is the link again: [JOIN NOW]
