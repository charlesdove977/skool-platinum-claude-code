# Task 2: About Page

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

**About Page **– this is your landing page where you’ll send traffic.

Its sole purpose is to convince people to join the community. That is done by answering 3 questions:

1. *What* is this community?
2. *Who* is it for?
3. *Why* should you join?

To answer those questions, add these to your about page:

- Short 2-5 minute video – template [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1X4pEQC29VGUMYgVoYyfoF5OiQe-eFnjn7zIQCymiq0o/edit?usp=sharing)
- Text description below the video – ([Template Option 1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/130dIL2HvspchkfHQIrGzrc1PoxL5sPWPie3MzJptW5w/edit?tab=t.0) + [Template Option 2](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mMiFuiSwGACAmYHLmvKCtsVmDYWBQgqB9BiY4LT27EU/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.gk3awgd40e2r))

**Pro tips:**

- Be your authentic self and display your personality – make it fun. People want to join a community, not a lecture hall
- Be specific about the outcome / transformation your community teaches

Things that do **not** belong on the About Page:

❌AI phrases that mean nothing:

- No fluff, just results
- Remove the guesswork
- Stop overthinking
- You don’t need more ___, you need to know what actually works

❌Your background / biography

❌External links to other websites

❌Large blocks or paragraphs of text

Things that **do** belong on the About Page:

✅Short, punchy bullet points

✅Specifically what’s offered inside the community (community, calls, etc.)

✅Success stories & testimonials
