# YouTube Channel Checklist

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

To maximize the amount of traffic that gets sent from your YouTube to your Skool, make sure you're following all of these:

- Is your Skool link the only link on your YT channel? (even having 2 drops your traffic ~50%)

![image 1](images/start-here/41-youtube-channel-checklist-1.png)

- Are you writing a daily Community Post that drives traffic to your community? (It doesn't always have to be a hard sell - a screenshot of a member post or community win with a link works great)
- Have you bulk updated all previous video descriptions to CTA to your skool? ([instructions here](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6249768?hl=en))
- Are you intentionally creating YouTube videos that attracts the type of person who would want your Skool offer?
- Does your community promotion feel like the natural next step from the video, or does it feel randomly inserted?
- Have you removed all links to other offers?
- Everywhere you have a written CTA (pinned comments, community posts, video descriptions), are you clearly explaining what's inside your community and why someone should join?
