# Community Growth Loop

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

This flowchart demonstrates everything you need to do to grow your skool.

![image 1](images/start-here/38-community-growth-loop-1.png)

> **Key takeaway:** promote your community using what's happening in your community.

### Here's how it works:

1. Promote your Skool by testing different CTAs to get new signups.
2. New members + you create activity inside your community through wins, live calls, networking, discussions, and learnings.
3. That activity becomes marketing material for future content.
4. Create content that features what's happening inside your group + shows people winning.
5. With that new content, test new CTAs, get more members, and repeat the cycle.



Now let's break it down step by step, starting with what's happening in your Skool:

![image 2](images/start-here/38-community-growth-loop-2.png)

When you show up everyday in your community, cool things will happen.

People will have wins, people will ask interesting questions, people will become friends with one another, people will have breakthroughs, people will meetup, and many more things you probably never expected will happen.

In Skoolers, for example, we've had people get married and even get skool tattoos.

All of this is now your marketing material.

> Best way to keep growing your community is simple: **share what's happening inside it.**

Why is this the best?

It creates FOMO (the fear of missing out).

The more your audience sees people winning and exciting things happening inside your community, the more they'll want to stop watching from the sidelines and join.

[YM Babie](https://www.instagram.com/ym_babie/?hl=en) ([Trading Bootcamp](https://www.skool.com/tradingbootcamp/about?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=social&utm_content=link_in_bio)) does this very well.

She posts 3-5 member wins on her IG story PER DAY.

After a week of following her on IG, followers have seen her members win 20+ times and the proof becomes undeniable.

That's what creates a must-join community.
