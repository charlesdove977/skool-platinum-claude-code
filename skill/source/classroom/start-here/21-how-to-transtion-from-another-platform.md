# How To Transtion From Another Platform

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

Some of you may be switching from Kajabi, Circle, Thinkific, etc.

Here's the best way to bring those paying members to your new skool

1. **Highlight the benefits. **Position your skool as a new exciting IMPROVEMENT to what they're currently getting: more engaged community experience, live calls, leaderboards, challenges, more course content, etc. If you position it just as a "platform change", it'll sound boring.
2. **Show conviction. **If you genuinely believe this is the best experience for your members, communicate that confidently. People are much more likely to follow your lead when they understand the benefits.
3. **Offer an exclusive bonus.** Give members a reason to move now by including something that's only available inside your Skool community, such as bonus training, office hours, templates, or exclusive content.
4. **Give a discounted rate for founders. **Tell the existing members that you're publicly launching this new community on X date at X price, but for the current subscribers - you guys get a special, discounted rate that won't be available to the public. Then give them a week or so to lock in that discounted rate before publicly launching to socials at the higher rate. This way, the existing members feel special and rewarded.
5. **Set a clear deadline. **Tell members exactly when your old community will stop being active, and remind them multiple times before that date. Clear deadlines drive action.
6. **Handle payments smoothly.** Here’s the trick to doing this… Tell your members to pay to join your new Skool, and that when they do you’ll refund their last payment on your old platform. They effectively receive some extra time at no additional cost, making the transition feel like a reward rather than an inconvenience.
7. **Someone doesn’t join? **You can keep their billing on your old platform and invite them for free to the new platform so that you don’t completely lose on their subscription
