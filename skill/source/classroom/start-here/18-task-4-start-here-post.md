# Task 4: Start Here post

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

You only need 1 post in your community to get started: the "Start Here" post.

At minimum, this should:

- Greet people into the community and remind them why they're here
- Ask them to write an introduction post to introduce themself

Feel free to add any additional onboarding instructions that your group calls for.
