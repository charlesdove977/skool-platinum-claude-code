# 🚀 Bonus: Growth Boost

_Start Here / _

Skool Growth Boost is how Skool gives you members.

This lesson shows you how to optimize your Skool so we can send you as much traffic and members as possible.

**Growth Boost works in two main ways:**

1. We run Meta and Instagram ads directly to your group.
2. We promote your group through Skool search and the Skool homepage.

The results can be staggering:

![image 1](images/start-here/44-bonus-growth-boost-1.png)



![image 2](images/start-here/44-bonus-growth-boost-2.png)



**so, how do you get similar results?**

**Step 1 - enable growth boost + set your bid amount**

- Go to **Settings → Discovery** and make sure Growth Boost is enabled. It's on by default.
- Set your bid. 30% is the default, but 70% is the most popular among people who change it.
- A higher bid can bring you more members, but at a lower profit margin.
- You pay no Growth Boost fees on customers you bring in yourself.

![image 3](images/start-here/44-bonus-growth-boost-3.png)

**Step 2 - optimize your cover image**

- Upload a cover image that follows [these best practices](https://www.skool.com/platinum-october/classroom/5fab3dee?md=6f72d389647440af9c56e6ad545e7e08)
- If you're not seeing Skool Network visitors or signups after a week, test a new variation.
Make a meaningful change, like a completely different headline or promise, not just a subtle color change.

**Step 3 - send traffic, convert members, retain them**

- Sending traffic to your About page that converts and then retains members month over month is the best way to get more Growth Boost traffic.

> **The goal of growth boost is to promote quality groups.**
> 
> Skool sends traffic to high quality groups that convert members and retain them month over month by delivering a stellar customer experience.
> 
> Focusing on sending traffic, converting that traffic, and retaining members is the best way to get more Growth Boost traffic.
