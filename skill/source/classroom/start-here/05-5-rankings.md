# 5 – Rankings

_Start Here / 0. Onboarding_

#### **How it works:**

- Leaderboard tracks your** MRR Growth:**
- (MRR Growth = New MRR - Churned MRR)
- Only **monthly + annual recurring revenue** will be tracked for the leaderboard
- Annual subscriptions are divided by 12 and added to your MRR
- ❌ One-time payments do not count
- ✅ High-ticket MRR does count for the leaderboard

> **⭐️ Pro Tip: **find people who have added a lot of MRR, and click their "audience" link to see how they're doing it! All the strategies are right here in front of you.
