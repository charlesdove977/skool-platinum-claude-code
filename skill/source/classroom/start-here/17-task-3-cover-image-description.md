# Task 3: Cover Image + Description

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

##### 🚨 Your cover image is the image we use to run ads and promote your community, so it's one of the most important assets you have.

Within 1-2 seconds, someone should be able to understand what your community is about and why they should join.

If your cover image isn't clear, your ads are much less likely to perform well, so they won't be given as much adspend and you'll get less traffic from skool.

Certain people's communities have, quite literally, blown up because they have the right cover image that converted so well, so take your time with this.

Here's 6 examples of highly performing cover images:

![image 1](images/start-here/17-task-3-cover-image-description-1.png)

**✅ A good cover image:**

- Has big bold text that communicates the benefit you get from joining the group
- Clearly conveys the theme of the group (tattooing, AI, health, etc.)
- Includes the creator's face
