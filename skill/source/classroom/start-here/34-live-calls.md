# Live Calls

_Start Here / 4. Delivery_

##### **How often to host a call + scheduling**

It's up to you how often you host live calls, but we generally recommend at least once per week.

Even if you don't know your exact schedule yet, make sure the next 3-4 months at least are populated with live calls scheduled.

You can always update them later.

What you want to avoid is someone joining your community, seeing an empty calendar, and leaving.

##### **What to do on the call**

Bringing members together face to face builds relationships, strengthens the community, and reduces churn. It also creates a consistent weekly rhythm that members can look forward to.

Here's some ideas for calls you can host:

- ❓ Q&A – Members ask questions - you answer them
- ☕ Coffee Chat – Hang out and connect together
- 🎤 Guest Speaker – Bring in an interview someone interesting
- 🛠️ Workshop – Build something together live
- 🔥 Hot Seats – Members get live feedback on their work / progress
- ⚡ Implementation Session – Everyone leaves with something completed
- 🧠 Masterclass – You teach / deep dive into one topic

> **⭐️ Pro tip: **Host a weekly giveaway – ask people to share a win or write a post in the community to win a gift card, 3 free months of access to the group, 1:1 time with you, etc. On your weekly call, screenshare and [spin a wheel](https://wheelofnames.com/) to pick a winner. Giving people something to look forward to and a reason to show up live is extremely effective.
