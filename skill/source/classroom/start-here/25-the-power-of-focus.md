# The Power of Focus

_Start Here / 3. Launch_

Imagine you're trying to start a fire...

with a magnifying glass.

But instead of holding it steady, you're constantly moving it around trying to heat up different twigs at the same time.

A little bit of heat here. A little bit of heat there.

Everything gets slightly warm, but nothing catches fire.

Then you stop moving it.

You pick a single spot and hold the magnifying glass there.

Not for 1 second, but for a while.

100% of the sun's energy is focused into one point.

Eventually, a spark appears and a fire is born.

Why did it spark this time?

Because you had these 2 factors present

1. **Concentration**: all energy pointing to 1 spot
2. **Time**: holding the focus long enough for results to appear

![image 1](images/start-here/25-the-power-of-focus-1.png)

##### **The same principle applies to building a skool community.**

When you have 10 links in your bio, 5 different CTAs in your content, and multiple places you're trying to send people...nobody really goes anywhere. 

Everything gets a little attention.

Nothing catches fire.

Instead, concentrate everything in one place.

Every post. Every story. Every video. Every CTA.

Point people to one destination and one destination only: your Skool 

Do that consistently, and eventually you'll get a spark.

Then a flame.

Then a fire.

Why do we recommend this? Because it simply works.

It's exactly what $100k/mo creators on skool already do to win.

So, if you want to build successful skool community, you should do the same.
