# Bulk Edit YouTube Descriptions

_Start Here / 3. Launch_

Your old YouTube videos are still getting views, so let's use that traffic to grow your Skool community.

YouTube lets you bulk edit the descriptions of all your videos in YouTube Studio.

[Instructions are here.](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6249768?hl=en)

With one update, your entire video library becomes a traffic source for your community.
