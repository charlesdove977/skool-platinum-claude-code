# The 6 Setup Steps

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

Here are the 6 simple tasks to set up your Skool

1. Determine your Positioning, Deliverables, and Pricing
2. Write your About Page
3. Add a Cover Image and Group Description
4. Write your "Start Here" post
5. Plan your Classroom
6. Schedule a Kick Off call

This should take roughly 2-4 hours.
