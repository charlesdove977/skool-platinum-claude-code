# ManyChat

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

A lot of people get tons of comments through ManyChat but very few conversions.

This module will show you why that happens and how to fix it.

**❌ The biggest mistake is being too generic.**

People post a Reel with a CTA like:

> "Comment GUIDE if you want more info!"

Then their ManyChat message says:

> "Thanks for commenting! Here's the info you requested."

The problem is people have no idea what they're getting.

So when they click the link and see it costs money, they don't convert. 



**✅ Here's what to do instead.**

First, identify the most valuable part of your community. What's the one thing most likely to make someone want to join? If you're not sure yet, make your best guess and test it.

Then create a Reel that naturally leads into that benefit.

When it's time for the CTA, be specific. Literally point the camera at your laptop and show the resource, the live call on the calendar, the challenge, or whatever you're offering, and clearly explain that it exists in your private community.

Then add urgency.

You verbally say in the reel:

> "For the next 25 people who join, I'm (insert special offer). Comment **INVITE** if you want to claim your spot."

*****they comment INVITE*******

Your ManyChat DM automation says:

> "Hey! I saw your comment. We only have a few spots left for (insert special offer). Want me to send you the invite link?"

Once they reply yes, send the link and reinforce the urgency.

> "Here's the invite link. Make sure to join soon if you want to (achieve valuable outcome that you offer) before spots sell out. Looking forward to seeing you inside!"

See the difference?

In the 2nd example, the follower knows:

- that they're joining a community
- what they're going to get inside the community
- that it's paid

They're sold before they even land on your about page.
