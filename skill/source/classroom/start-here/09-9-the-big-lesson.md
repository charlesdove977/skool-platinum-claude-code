# 9 – The Big Lesson

_Start Here / 0. Onboarding_

After studying dozens of $100k /month educational creators, the pattern became very clear:

**They went all-in on skool.**



#### What does "all-in" actually mean?

Hormozi defines commitment as the elimination of alternatives.

![image 1](images/start-here/09-9-the-big-lesson-1.png)

So we can define all-in as the elimination of **all** alternatives.



All $100k /month educators we studied:

- Made promoting their skool their #1 focus
- Added their skool as their only link in bio on all social platforms
- Regularly promoted their skool in their content
- Did not get distracted by selling multiple offers



It is remarkable the progress you make when you focus 100% of your effort on one thing.

So the question is... are you ready to go all-in?
