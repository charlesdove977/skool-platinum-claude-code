#### **Instagram Story:**

The recommended story sequence for Instagram is** Hook -> Offer -> CTA -> Close**

> **Important: **do NOT just make a story with the link to your skool. Instagram reduces the reach of stories with links, so less people will see it.

**Hook **= get them to stop scrolling, excite them for what you'll reveal next

**Offer **=** **share what's happening in your group and why it will benefit them

**CTA **=** **Using [ManyChat,](https://manychat.com/?source=9d4342ea2049&ps_partner_key=OWQ0MzQyZWEyMDQ5&ps_xid=jFh5xcrURitPkA&gsxid=jFh5xcrURitPkA&gspk=OWQ0MzQyZWEyMDQ5&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23896183671&gbraid=0AAAABDyJOEXl_02-8xqp4XR5ZzNMELXi4&gclid=Cj0KCQjwrs7RBhDuARIsAIVfBD3nm8T_xrcF8KBR5Xv1ZdUPXh2Hk8h3VSljSiKxStESd7ki2sjURugaAvSsEALw_wcB) ask people to Comment a keyword for access (creates buying intent)

**Close **= Make the final pitch via DM automation and share link to your skool.

In your automated ManyChat message, highlight that you're currently offering a discounted, founding member rate that will increase on X date.

#### **Link in Bio:**

This one's pretty simple.

Update your IG link in bio to be your skool community url: [skool.com/yourgroup](http://skool.com/yourgroup)

Make your skool link your **1 and only link**

Multiple links confuse audiences, hurting traffic and conversion

Update your bio to call out the link: 👇Join my ___ community,  ⬇️ Train with me, etc.

#### **DMs:**

As people respond to your Stories and Reels, you'll naturally get DMs asking about the community.

Don't ignore them.

Start conversations. Answer questions. Sell the community.

Early on, these one-on-one conversations are incredibly valuable. They not only help you get your first members, but also help you better understand:

- Common objections
- Hesitations
- What people actually want

Use those insights to strengthen your offer over time.
