# Launch first, build later

_Start Here / 1. Mindset_

Listen to this clip from [Nick Saraev](https://www.skool.com/makerschool/about?utm_source=skooldotcom&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=discovery_search_group_link) - he makes $250k/mo with his skool.

> The question isn't "how do I get this 100% right today?" it's "how can I set myself up with a habit that I can do 80% right everyday for a year" * - ***Nick Saraev**

The **biggest mistake **skoolers make when creating communities is they spend months by themselves creating a 10-part course *thinking *it's what their audience wants.

The problem is what creators *think *their audience wants is usually wrong.

- The **recommended Skool launch strategy** is to launch your skool as a MVP (minimum viable product). After you launch, you build your course live with your community in response to their direct needs week by week. This way, you know exactly what to build for them because they're telling you what their problems are everyday live.
