# 3 Phases of Engagement

_Start Here / 4. Delivery_

Now that you know the boots-on-the-ground tactical steps to take, let's zoom out a bit.

What's the goal of engagement?

The goal of engagement is for members to **identify** with the community.

When someone aligns their identity with your community, they don't churn.

Follow these 3 phases of engagement to transition your new signups into committed members of your community who identify with it.

####   
**Phase 1: Introduction (Week 1)**

- **Initial DM:** Start a 1:1 convo, welcome them, and learn about who they are
- **Kickoff Call:** Host a live call 24-48 hours after launching to set the tone and create early familiarity
- **Welcome Thread:** Encourage each new member to write a short intro post
- **Welcome Post Tags:** Personally tag users in the welcome post who’ve signed up so they feel seen

#### **Phase 2: Building Trust & Daily Buy-in (Weeks 2-4)**

- **Daily Rhythm:** Publish a daily update or recap to create a predictable cadence for the community.
- **Spotlight Posts:** Regularly pin a member win, insight, or helpful contribution to reward great posts and incentivize others (don't use pinned posts for **ONLY** logistical announcements, that's boring!)
- **Accountability Partners:** Create a post where you tag everyone into pairs of two. Encourage them reach out to one another to stay accountable
- **Check-In Messages:** Reach out to members who have gone quiet and ask how they’re doing and re-engage them
- **Redirect to the Community:** When someone shares wins or questions privately, encourage them to post it publicly so the group benefits

#### **Phase 3: Identity & Culture (Week 4+)**

- **Promote Power Users:** Consider promoting your most trusted, power users to become moderators.
- **Shared Humor:** Lean into inside jokes and lighthearted interactions; fun strengthens community bonds.

The goal of all of these is to have your members undergo an **Identity Shift **- when they start to see themselves as part of the community, not just visitors.
