# Big Idea

_Start Here / 4. Delivery_

Congrats on launching!

To get members, there's only 1 thing to do: drive traffic.

That was the "Big Idea" of the Launch lesson.

Similarly, in "Delivery", there's really only 1 thing that matters: **show up everyday**

![image 1](images/start-here/31-big-idea-1.png)



When you show up everyday, here's what happens:

- You develop personal relationships with your members --> they don't churn
- You understand their needs / problems / desires more deeply --> your offer gets better
- You help people get results --> your proof gets stronger + conversion rate goes up

![image 2](images/start-here/31-big-idea-2.png)



If your activity chart looks like this, people will notice.

> **⭐️ If you can't be bothered to show up everyday, why would your members?**
