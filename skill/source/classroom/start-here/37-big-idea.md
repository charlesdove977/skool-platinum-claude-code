# Big Idea

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

If your launch didn't bring in hundreds of members, that's okay.

In fact, that's normal.

Most successful Skool communities don't grow overnight.

They grow steadily over time.

For example, let's say you launch and get 10 members at $50/month. That's $500 MRR.

Most people would freak out and consider that a flop.

> "I have 100K followers but only got 10 signups... something must be horribly wrong!"

Now imagine you average just 6 new members per day.

Just 6 signups. Every day.

After 150 days, you'd be at roughly $35K MRR (and that includes churn)

At that point, not only have you surpassed the Platinum milestones...

...but you're in the top 1% of income worldwide.

And that's with just 6 new members per day.

Imagine what happens if you outperform that.

---

The lesson is simple: small, consistent gains compound into big results.

**The key is to stay in the game long enough for those results to happen.**

Most people quit way too soon.

But you're in Platinum because we know that's not you.
