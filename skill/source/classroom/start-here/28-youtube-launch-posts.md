# YouTube Launch Posts

_Start Here / 3. Launch_

There's 4 ways we're going to convert your YouTube subs --> paying members on your skool.

1. In-Video CTA
2. Pinned Comments
3. Update All Video Descriptions
4. Channel Link

#### **In-Video CTA:**

To launch your community, add a 30-60 second CTA to the end of your next YouTube video.

In your CTA:

- Tell them your vision for this community / why you're launching it
- Tell them what's inside the group and how that will benefit them
- Literally show the community (screenshots, video walkthrough)
- Create urgency (e.g., first 25 people to join get founding member rate + 1:1 call)

> To make your CTA successful, it must be framed as the **natural next step*** *from the content they're watching.

To fully understand this concept, watch these two examples:

**Example#1 - ****[Nolan Gouveia](https://youtu.be/4ItZFsE_-go?si=Vc5cvEXPV_VXOla4&t=489)**

**Example #2 -** **[Nick Saraev](https://www.youtube.com/watch?si=NZ3UyxuADVde2yQ5&t=1355&v=jBF48jNWPJE&feature=youtu.be)**

Notice how for both of them, the CTA is positioned as the **natural next step** for somebody who just enjoyed the free YouTube video and now wants more.

The **most common mistake** is just tacking on a random CTA to a video that didn't tee it up properly.

The way to do this is to specifically highlight:

1. WHAT is in your community
2. HOW it will help them
3. WHO it is for

#### **Update Video Descriptions:**

Your old YouTube videos are still getting views, so let's use that attention to drive traffic to your Skool.

Just like you did for your launch video, update the description of every video to include your Skool link at the top.

**⭐️ Pro Tip: **you can update all descriptions in bulk with one click in YouTube Studio. Instructions are [here](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6249768?hl=en).

With one update, your entire video library becomes a traffic source for your community.

![image 2](images/start-here/28-youtube-launch-posts-2.png)

Update your channel link with your skool link: [skool.com/yourgroup](http://skool.com/yourgroup)
