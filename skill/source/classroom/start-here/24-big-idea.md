# Big idea

_Start Here / 3. Launch_

This module covers how to launch your Skool community.

But, there's really only **1 thing** that matters: traffic

Most people drastically underestimate how much traffic they need. The average $100K+/month Skool community gets **43,000 about page views per month**.

Each of you has 100K+ followers, so reaching that level of traffic is achievable.

**Bottom line:** make a great offer and drive as much traffic to it as possible.
