# Daily Flow

_Start Here / 4. Delivery_

Based on your offer and situation, what you do in your group everyday might look slightly different from person to person.

But, at minimum, here's the recommended flow of what to do everyday when you log-in to your community:

##### 1) Clear your DMs

Every day, people will DM you questions, respond to your auto-DM, and much more.

Answer their questions, but also engage with them!

Ask them questions, get to know them.

Personal relationships are built in the DMs where things feel more private.

Building personal relationships with your members = less churn

Even responding with a simple emoji (🤝💪🙌🏻) or gif goes a long way in making people feel heard and personally supported.

##### 2) Respond to community posts

Catch up on the conversation the community has been having.

See what posts have been made and who has responded.

This is one of the most valuable assets you have - **people will literally tell you exactly what they're confused about, what they want to achieve, and more. **

All you have to do is... show up and read it!

- If a lot of people are confused about how to get started, or where to find a specific module, that's a good sign to make that more clear as part of your onboarding.
- If a lot of people are struggling with something in particular, then you may want to consider making a course module that addresses this problem.

> Hopefully it's becoming clear why the "launch, then build" philosophy is so effective.
> 
> Imagine joining a community with 100 course videos, but only 10 are relevant to your situation.
> 
> Now imagine joining a community, sharing your biggest challenge, and seeing the owner create a new module the following week specifically to solve it.
> 
> Which experience feels better? Which makes you more confident that this is a community worth staying in?
> 
> The answer is obvious.
> 
> Even though you've already launched, you're *always *building. 

**Important: **it's okay to not be the first to respond to every single community post! You want to give the community room to breathe - let others chime in and engage with one another. This will help reduce dependency on you over time.

Sam is the CEO of Skool, so he's busy (just like you).

Nonetheless, everyday, he finds time to respond to people's posts, answer questions, and encourage people!

Take a look at his profile [here](https://www.skool.com/@sam?g=skoolers) and you'll get a good sense of what daily touchpoints look like

##### 3) Make a daily post

The best communities give members something consistent to look forward to.

This is why live calls are so effective - everyone knows they'll happen at the same time each week.

You should create a similar habit with your posting schedule to the community.

For example, in **[Skoolers](https://www.skool.com/skoolers)**, we publish **[Skool News](https://www.skool.com/skoolers/skool-news-64-the-new-skool-game)** every week with product updates, community highlights, and shoutouts. It's become part of our members' weekly routine.

It's so ingrained in the culture of skoolers that some people around the world even stay up till 3AM just to see it live.

Even though Skoolers is a weekly update, for your group that you're just launching...

**you should make a daily post to keep people engaged.**



### Here's 5 post ideas to choose from for your daily post:

- Engagement Post – simple, fun, easy for people to participate in ([example](https://www.loom.com/i/fc1255ca71b84b46ba8bf060b3ab1a74))
- Top Posts Round Up – screenshot 3 best posts of the week
- Top Wins Round Up – top 3 biggest wins of the week ([example](https://www.loom.com/i/28920ec5ae0a4e569d8c2c48a7447917))
- Value Add – take a top performing recent piece of content, put it into a post format with a question ([example](https://www.loom.com/i/8568d30d219046258a699da009d61a87))
- Call Recap – share key takeaways, spark discussion, share recording ([example](https://www.loom.com/i/9c670a098b8a46089ef13a16128005cc))

Here's 2 more examples of skoolers who execute daily posts very well:

**[Mikey Caloca's](https://www.skool.com/@mikey)** daily "Good Morning" post:

Every morning, Mikey shares upcoming live calls, celebrates member wins, wishes members happy birthday, and posts community updates. It's a simple daily ritual that keeps people engaged. Everyone knows when they wake up, they'll have a Mikey Good Morning post to look forward to (that they might even be featured in).

![image 4](images/start-here/33-daily-flow-4.png)

Here's another great example from **[Alan Powell](https://www.skool.com/youre-awesome/about)**. Every day, he posts "Dear Awesomes Daily", which includes a question of the day, a short video, community updates, a daily focus, and more.

![image 5](images/start-here/33-daily-flow-5.png)

A simple way to do this is to recap what happened in the community over the last 24 hours, highlight member wins, and get people excited for what's coming next, whether that's live calls, giveaways, challenges, or other upcoming events.

> **⭐️ Pro Tip: **Include a short video in your daily post, like Alan and Mikey do. *Seeing* you every day helps members build a relationship with you, strengthens the community, and reduces churn.
