# Task 5: Plan your Classroom

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

There are 3 main use cases of the Classroom.

Feel free to implement any and all that apply to what you’re building with your skool.

**1) Course Content **– pre recorded lessons. Focus on teaching and delivering value over production value. These don’t need fancy editing.

> **Important: **If you already have a course, that's great. Upload that course into your classroom.
> 
> If you don't have a course, you do **NOT** need to build one before launching.
> 
> It's better to launch with your MVP (minimum viable product), and build the classroom live around the needs of your community.
> 
> Nick Saraev, and other top skool earners, spent a few hours building their community MVP, didn't overthink their launch, and grew them live, post-launch *with* their community.
> 
> Your members will appreciate this because:
> 
> 1) You're building in direct response to their specific needs
> 
> 2) They'll feel like co-creators of the community, which builds identity / decreases churn

![image 1](images/start-here/19-task-5-plan-your-classroom-1.png)

**2) Recorded Live Calls **– give your community members a chance to watch the call if they couldn’t make it live

![image 2](images/start-here/19-task-5-plan-your-classroom-2.png)

**3) Extra Resources **– if you have any worksheets, PDFs, excel files, etc. you can add them as downloadable resources in the Classroom
