# Task 1: Positioning, Deliverables, Pricing

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

The first thing we'll want to determine is...

- Why would people join your group?
- What do people get access to inside? (deliverables)
- How is your group priced?

Thankfully, this is very simple.

By the end of this module, you'll have

✅ Your Positioning Statement

✅ Your Deliverables

✅ Your Price

Here's an example:

##### **Positioning Statement:**

[Maker School](https://www.skool.com/makerschool/about) is the fastest & most affordable way for AI freelancers to get their first client for AI services through a day-by-day accountability system.

##### **Deliverables: **

✅ $21K in IMMEDIATE software discounts to platforms you use every day

✅ >40 copy-paste templates/scripts for content, lead gen, and sales 📦

✅ 3 underground lead gen methods to find & close high-ticket clients FAST

✅ Live coaching—ask Nick anything & get direct feedback

✅ Member map — find who lives near you and make friends

✅ Community  — connect with other entrepreneurs and build together

##### **Pricing: **

$184 / month or $1,104 / year

### Step #1: Positioning

First, open up a blank Google Doc, and write out the answers to these questions:

1) Who is your target audience? Who are you helping?

2) What outcome do you deliver and how does their life change as a result of it?

3) Generally, how do you help them achieve that outcome?

3) Why should they learn this from you? What's your proof?

With the answers to these questions, we now want to write your positioning statement.

##### But first, here's some great examples:.

- [✅ Project Bellplex ](https://www.skool.com/projectbellplex/about)helps men to build functional strength, conditioning and mobility, by following a kettlebell-based at-home training program.
- [✅ ](https://www.skool.com/projectbellplex/about)[Blue Collar Biz Accelerator](https://www.skool.com/bluecollar/about) helps home service business owners never run out of leads, hire the right people, and scale their business through 1:1 implementation support of their proven sales, marketing, and hiring system.
- [✅ ](https://www.skool.com/projectbellplex/about)[The Lady Change](https://www.skool.com/theladychange/about) helps women aged 40-75 to lose weight, get healthier and regain their confidence by direct coaching, joint friendly workouts, and hormone-friendly meal plans.

Notice how these sentences are short, but packed with information. Every word is specific to their target audience and their needs. Zero filler words.

##### 🚨 If your positioning statement sounds anything like this, it's not specific enough:

- ❌ I help ambitious men level up in life through mindset, discipline, and personal growth.

- ❌ I help entrepreneurs unlock their full potential by building better habits, improving their mindset, and surrounding themselves with the right people.
- ❌ I help high performers create a life they love by improving their health, wealth, relationships, and purpose.

Notice how these are very generic? They don't say much, and they don't speak to a specific audience.

### Step #3: Pricing

First find your niche below to determine what your general price range is.

Second, consider offers you've made in the past.

If your previous offers **sold well in the lower end of your range**, price your skool there.

If your previous offers **sold well in the upper end of your range**, price your skool there.

If you've **never made previous offers**, price your skool in the middle of your range.

These optimal price ranges are based on the top earning communities in each niche.

**🎨 Hobbies **– $29 - 55/month

**🎸 Music **– $39 - 75/month

**💰 Money **– $65 - 200/month

**🙏 Spirituality **– $30 - 70/month

**💻 Tech **– $70 - 110/month

**🥕 Health **– $30 - 70/month

**⚽️ Sports **– $50 - 170/month

**📚 Self-Improvement **– $50 - 120/month

**❤️ Relationships **– $35 - 100/month

> **Important: **if you raise your price, existing members will remain locked in at their current monthly rate. Reserving a limited # of spots as the "founding rate" is a great way to drive urgency for your launch.

For **Annual Subscriptions: **it's best to offer a 20-40% discount to the monthly price.

Lastly, your goal is to hit $100k / month in MRR. So consider this:

- It takes 500 members at $200/mo to hit $100k/mo
- It takes 1,000 members at $100/mo to hit $100k/mo
- It takes 2,000 members at $50/mo to hit $100k/mo
- It takes 4,000 members at $25/mo to hit $100k/mo

**🎉 Congrats! **You now have your positioning statement, deliverables, and price.
