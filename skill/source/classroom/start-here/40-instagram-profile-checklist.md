# Instagram Profile Checklist

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

To maximize the amount of traffic that gets sent from your Instagram to your Skool, make sure you're following all of these:

- Is your Skool link the only link in your IG bio? (even having 2 drops your traffic ~50%)
- Have you removed CTAs that compete with your Skool? (Newsletter signups, Discord, Facebook groups, affiliate links, etc. all create friction.)
- Are you posting a daily Story that drives traffic to your community? (It doesn't always have to be a hard sell - a screenshot of a member post or community win with a link works great)
- Do you have a pinned post on your profile promoting your community? ([see this example](https://www.instagram.com/p/DapvhawiuRj/?igsh=MW0xM211MXN3emtjbQ%3D%3D))
- Have you made an IG Story Highlight on your profile with all previous stories about your Skool?
- Have you replied to everyone who has DM'd you asking for help and directed them to your Skool?
- Have you replied to post comments from people asking for help and directed them to your Skool?
- Does someone understand why they should join within 10 seconds of landing on your profile? (Your bio, pinned posts, and recent content should all reinforce the same core promise.)
- Would someone who binge-watches your last 10 posts naturally conclude that joining your community is the next step? (your content should build one consistent narrative rather than feeling like unrelated tips.)
- Is your community visible in your content, not just mentioned?** **(literally show the community tab, discussions, live calls, member interactions, and wins so people can see what's inside and develop FOMO)
- Does your Instagram bio point to the link and clearly highlight a benefit of joining your community?
