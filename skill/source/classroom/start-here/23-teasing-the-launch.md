# Teasing the Launch

_Start Here / 2. Setup_

Before you launch on socials, you want to signal to your audience that something is coming.

Something is coming = anticipation. 

Anticipation = eager buyers on launch day.

Eager buyers on launch day = lots of signups and $.

---

##### Here's how to tease on Instagram:

- Create an IG story of you speaking sharing that you have a big announcement coming
- CTA to join your Instagram broadcast channel for updates on the launch
- [Here](https://help.instagram.com/783859809732797) are instructions on creating an IG broadcast channel
- Day by day, drip more and more information about the launch (don't give it all away at once)
- Example:
- Day 1: Big announcement is coming 👀 stay tuned. Join the broadcast channel for live updates
      - Day 2: If you're (target audience), I'm building something for you...
      - Day 3: I've been seeing a lot of people struggling with (painpoint), and finally have something to help you (achieve valuable outcome)
      - Day 4: Here's a sneak peek at what's inside... 👀 One thing you'll get inside is (deliverable #1).  
This will help you (specific benefit/outcome).  
And that's just one small part of what's coming...
      - Day 5: You'll also get (deliverable #2) and(deliverable #3) — all designed to help you (achieve valuable outcome).  
Tomorrow, I'll finally show you everything and open the doors.

##### Here's how to tease on YouTube:

- With YouTube, use the exact same day-by-day drip strategy as Instagram, except post this as a YouTube community post.
- If you have a video to post during the teaser period, include the same messaging in that video
