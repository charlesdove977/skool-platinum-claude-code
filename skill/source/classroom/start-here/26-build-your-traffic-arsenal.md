# Build Your Traffic Arsenal

_Start Here / 3. Launch_

Before we start, let's take stock of all the ways you could generate traffic.

Often people will launch and then say: *"I forgot I had this whole 50K email list I could've used!"*

This exercise will prevent that.

![image 1](images/start-here/26-build-your-traffic-arsenal-1.png)

This table above outlines traffic sources that you may have that you might've forgotten about.

It also includes our **minimum recommended posting schedule for a successful launch.**

Here's how to use it:

1. **[Click here to create a personal copy of the workbook.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1alukftmewuWztBfylYrNEDP4eHTdbAeWdOy9chKxIJY/copy)**
2. Go through each traffic source and remove any rows for platforms you don't use. Keep only the ones you have.
3. The checked boxes show our recommended posting cadence for a successful launch (for example, one Story every other day with a promotional CTA). Feel free to check additional boxes to build out your launch schedule.
4. **Execute**: everyday - look at what you've committed to posting and post it

> **Important: **We recommend keeping the default schedule—posting less may reduce your launch results.
