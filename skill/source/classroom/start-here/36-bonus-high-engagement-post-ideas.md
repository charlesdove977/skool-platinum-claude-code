# Bonus: High Engagement Post Ideas

_Start Here / 4. Delivery_

Here are some simple, proven post ideas to boost engagement in your community.

We know these work because we analyzed top communities and identified the posts that consistently get the most engagement.

#### **Getting Started / Networking Posts**

##### Introduce Yourself! 🌟

Welcome! Introduce yourself below by sharing where you're from and what you're hoping to achieve inside the community. We'd love to get to know you.

---

##### Share Your Socials Below!

Drop your social links below so everyone can connect, support each other, and stay in touch outside the community. Feel free to share any platform you'd like.

---

##### Welcome! Introduce Yourself + Share Your Workspace 🎉

Tell us where you're from, share a photo of your workspace, and one fun fact about yourself. It's a great way to get to know the community.

---

##### Where Are You From? Connect with Local Members 🌎

Share your city, country, and preferred language to connect with members near you. If you'd like, include a social profile so others can reach out.

---

##### 📌 Kickoff Thread: Share Your Commitment

Introduce yourself by sharing why you joined, where you want to be in six months, and one goal you're committed to achieving. Use this thread to find an accountability partner and support each other along the way.





#### **Discussion Prompting:**

##### Your First Time Using [Niche]

What was your first experience with **[niche]**? Share how you got started, what you thought at the time, and how your perspective has changed since then.

---

##### Do Hard Things 💪

Building your business is important, but so is your health. What's one health or fitness goal you're working toward this year?

---

##### Biggest Lesson Learned 💡

What's one lesson you wish you had learned sooner in **[niche]**? Share the advice that would've saved you the most time, money, or frustration.

---

##### Biggest Win This Week 🎉

What's your biggest win from this week, no matter how big or small? Celebrate your progress and inspire someone else to keep going.

---

##### One Tool You Can't Live Without 🛠️

If you could only use **one** tool, app, or resource for **[niche]**, what would it be and why? Share your favorite so everyone can discover something new.

---

##### Advice for Beginners 🚀

Imagine someone is starting **[niche]** today. What's the one piece of advice you'd give them to help them succeed faster?





#### **Challenge Announcements**

##### Build Challenge of the Week ⚡

 This week's challenge is live! Build the prompt, workflow, workout, design, or assignment below and submit it in the comments before **Friday at midnight**. I'll pick my favorite submission and send the winner a **free 1:1 coaching call** **+ free lifetime access to the group**

---

##### Feedback Friday 👀 

Post your latest project, landing page, video, or work-in-progress below. Give feedback to **at least two other members**, and I'll personally review a few of my favorite submissions.

---

##### Speed Challenge ⏱️

You have **30 minutes** to complete this week's challenge. Share your finished result in the comments before **Sunday**. One random participant will win a **free month of membership**.

---

##### Before & After Challenge 📈

Share your **before and after** results from implementing this week's lesson. Whether it's numbers, screenshots, photos, or progress updates, post them below. The most impressive transformation will be featured on the next live call. 

---

##### Community Vote Challenge 🏆

Complete this week's challenge and post your submission in the comments. The community will vote by liking their favorites, and the **top-voted submission** by next week's call wins a special prize.





#### **Miscellaneous **

##### Your Biggest Mistake 🤦

What's the biggest mistake you've made in **[niche]** so far? Share what happened and what you'd do differently so everyone can learn from it.

---

##### Build in Public 📈

Comment one thing you're building this week. Come back before Friday and reply to your own comment with whether you finished it.

---

##### $100 Challenge 💵

If I gave you **$100** to spend on improving your business, fitness, or skill, what would you buy and why?

---

##### Unpopular Opinion 🌶️

Share an unpopular opinion about **[niche]**. Bonus points if you can explain why you believe it.

---

##### Myths That Need to Die ☠️

What's one piece of advice everyone repeats in **[niche]** that you think is completely wrong?
