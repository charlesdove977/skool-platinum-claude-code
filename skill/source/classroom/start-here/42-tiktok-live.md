# TikTok Live

_Start Here / 5. Promotion_

**TikTok Live** is an extremely effective way to get **many paid signups quickly**.

For example:

- [Samuel G](https://www.skool.com/creativeowners/about?utm_source=skooldotcom&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=discovery_search_group_link) gets 50+ signups every TikTok Live
- [Mikey Caloca](https://www.skool.com/mikeysmoneyhouse) gets 20+ signups every TikTok Live (example above)

They work well because you get infront of people instantly and can build trust in real-time.

If you want to learn how to do this yourself, **[click here to watch the full strategy breakdown](https://www.skool.com/platinum-october/classroom/3c0d8b16?md=53a9852ba2754cc096367c757688d153)**

### TLDR:

**structure of the live:**

1) Teach a specific lesson: provide 1-2 hours of free value without pitching

2) Ask who wants help and step by step guidance

3) Share your skool as the next natural step.

**how to keep viewers watching + build buying intent**

- Start teaching and engaging immediately. Never wait for more viewers to join.
- Avoid jargon. Explain everything with simple words, so anyone can understand it instantly.
- Address common doubts / objections and use visual proof to make your argument.
- Stand during your live to improve your energy, voice, and body language.
