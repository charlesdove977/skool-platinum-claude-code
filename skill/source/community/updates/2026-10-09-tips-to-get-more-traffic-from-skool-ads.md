# Tips to get more traffic from skool ads

1\) Connect your socials so Skool can run ads . This is less a tip and more —>make sure u do that.
2\) Push to your organic audience so the group has some activity. We won’t push an empty group.
3\) Add in an annual payment option. The larger the discount the better. The sweet spot for the highest performing groups is around 40% off for annual.
4\) Slide the revshare up to start \(if you want\). And once it starts working you can dial it back to find the sweet spot. More like—>maximize to get traffic & growth then optimize for profit.
5\) Make a great cover photo with your face on it. See the discovery page for how to do it.
6\) Promise an outcome, not a community. “First client in 5 days” “Automate your email in 30min” not “join my marketing community.”
7\) Have a VSL + a compelling offer + proof in your about page. FOLLOW NOMAD.
8\) Make your CTAs to your community be about the promise \(point 6\) not the community. “If you want to get your first client in 5 days click here”
9\) More CTAs in more ways. Once you have a winning one, you can keep repeating it. \(I have a friend who makes the same story every week to sell his fitness coaching. It’s been multiple years.\)
10\) Go all in. Go live. Send emails. Post across multiple platforms. Ask people what they want. Give them bonuses for joining. Do whatever it takes to win - and you’ll be more likely to.
