# Engagement boost! 

I was having a hard time getting people to post their wins.
I posted an announcement saying everyone who posts a deal they have done gets entered into a monthly raffle for a pair of AirPods. They have to verify it's an actual deal they've done. After 6 months there is another raffle for an all-inclusive trip out to our office.

Immediately after posting, people who I've never seen posting in the community dropped the deals they have been doing. Every month now, there will be a live where I'll have a spin wheel for the AirPods giveaway. Every 6 months, I'll do a live where we spin it and see who wins the trip out to our office.

This doesn't interfere with terms and conditions or anything, does it?
