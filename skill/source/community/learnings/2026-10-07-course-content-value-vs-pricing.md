# Course Content Value vs Pricing 

We initially planned to price our course at €509 one-time, but after joining the community we're noticing most people run monthly subscriptions at $49–$99/month. We currently have 20–25 hours of structured course content already built. For those running the subscription model — what is the typical course volume you provide at these price points, and what keeps members subscribed month to month beyond the initial course content?

## Comments

- **Mark Novikov** (, 2 likes): People typically come for the course but stay because of the live, ongoing support.
  
  Because of that: we typically recommend having a weekly Q&A or live call on the calendar, as well as, of course, showing up in your group every day to talk to people, answer questions, hang out, etc.
  
  As long as people feel the community is active and helpful, they will stick around.
