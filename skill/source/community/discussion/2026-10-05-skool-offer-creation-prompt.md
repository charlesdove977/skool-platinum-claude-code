# Skool Offer Creation Prompt

I just want to quickly highlight this: if you go into the classroom > help section, there's this amazing little prompt to help you create your offer. I already thought I had a pretty good offer, but I copied the prompt, plus my existing offer into my existing Claude project, and said, "Look at this and look at my current offer. Have I missed anything?" It did an amazing job of finding the gaps and helping me refine things that I'd be missing. I highly recommend it. I just wanted to put it here, as it's hidden in the help section in Classroom, which you might not find.
