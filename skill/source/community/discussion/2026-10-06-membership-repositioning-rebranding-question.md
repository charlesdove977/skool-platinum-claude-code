# Membership Repositioning/Rebranding Question

Hey everyone! Excited to be here and already feeling inspired by all of your shares. I’d love some insight and feedback on a membership repositioning question.

I’ve had a membership platform on Kajabi for the past 2 years, but I haven’t loved the interface, so I’m excited to move everything over to Skool!

My offer is called HealthX. Originally, the positioning was geared more toward people dealing with chronic health issues—helping them take control of their health by identifying and addressing root causes. It was very much positioned through a functional medicine lens.

After testing different messaging with my community, I’ve decided I want to pivot the positioning more toward longevity and being intentional with how you age.

The new audience is more health-conscious: someone who isn’t necessarily sick, but knows they could feel better, wants to age well, and needs more clarity, structure, and consistency to actually live in a way that supports their long-term health.

My question is: How important is the name of the membership when making a positioning shift like this?

I’ve already marketed HealthX / HealthX Root Cause to more than 100,000 people over the years. Do you think people will see “HealthX” and automatically associate it with the previous offer, even if the messaging, branding, and positioning are clearly different?

Essentially, I’m wondering whether this pivot warrants an entirely new name—or whether I can keep the equity I’ve already built around HealthX and simply reposition it.

The content itself will probably remain about 70% the same; it’s really the audience, framing, and desired outcome that are evolving.

Would love to hear how you’d think about this. Thank you!
