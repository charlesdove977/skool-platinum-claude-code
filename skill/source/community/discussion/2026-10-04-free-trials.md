# Free Trials?

Hey people, what's the general consensus on Free Trials? Good to implement?

EDIT: Took a look at some of the top ranking communities here, most of which use Free Trials, so I think I got my answer 😂. Best practice on using it effectively would be appreciated however :\)

## Comments

- **Member ab17** (, 1 likes): Check out Samuel's interview in the classroom; he says no to free trials because when people don't have money on the line, they don't have anything keeping them accountable to doing the work and often people's cards get declined or have some issue moving from free to paid.
  
  That said, I've seen it done well elsewhere. How are you thinking about using it?
- **Member d614** (, 1 likes): I was literally just thinking about this this morning and trying to decide this myself. My impression from looking at what others have done and in interviews is that when you're starting out, it's best not to have a free option. Once you've got a well-established paid community, have a free one that you can send people to, with a clear link to the paid one, so they can see lots of people in there and that it's being active.
- **Member a3b5** (, 2 likes): i do not have a large community so take this with a grain of salt. My most engaged members have all come from a free trial, very skeptical, and mostly with plans to cancel, but fell in love with the community and are now massive community builders for me. I've asked them, and they said they would not have tried it without a free trial. I also need to mention the caveat that I do not have a good outcome statement, and I'm still trying to refine my marketing. This could just be because of the situation that I'm in, but that's my two cents.
