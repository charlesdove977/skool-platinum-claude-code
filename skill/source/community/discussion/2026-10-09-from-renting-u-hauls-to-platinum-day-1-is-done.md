# From Renting U-Hauls to Platinum. Day 1 Is Done.

What’s up, everybody. I’m Tanner. My wife Jackie and I own JT Junk Solutions in Member 37fe. Two years ago we were renting U-Hauls. Today we run two trucks and a full crew, and we coach junk removal owners inside JT’s Inner Circle on Skool.

Quick background on where we’re at.

For the last 90 days we’ve run the group as a low-ticket offer: lifetime access, starting at $149 and moving up to $249. That was our Grand Slam Offer, and it works as the front end of our high-ticket Partner Program. It’s been working well.
Since getting accepted into Platinum, we switched to an MRR model with a 7-day free trial.

Here’s what Day 1 looked like:
[ul][li]Got through 32% of the classroom content[li]Reshot our VSL for the About page and added updated social proof[li]Made our first post in our community announcing we’re competing[li]Posted the first video in a short-form series tracking our progress[li]Emailed our list of about 3,000[li]Updated every social platform to push people into the new group

Now my question.
We’ve always found it hard to get people to commit to a monthly model, and I’m worried about churn. Yesterday we had about 7 people sign up, and a couple of them canceled almost immediately.
I’m extremely confident in what’s inside the group, especially for someone who’s serious about building and scaling a junk removal company. I don't believe the content isn’t the problem. I want to make sure the first experience doesn’t lose people before they ever see it.

For those of you who’ve cracked this: what are the 2 or 3 basics that made the biggest difference in reducing early churn for you?

Appreciate every one of you. We’re going to run this challenge with extreme violence.
