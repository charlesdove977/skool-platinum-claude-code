# Got in! Quick question about having 3 Skool groups 🙋‍♀️

I got the text Friday that I was accepted. Thank you! I've applied every time I saw the ad. This is exactly what my business needs to go to the next level. I've been waking up at 4am just to get through the classroom.
My question:
I run a private homeschool program on Skool, split into 3 groups on purpose:
[ol:1][li]Community + Coaching: resources and support for parents. Unlimited seats.[li]Co-op: a real private school with live classes. Limited seats.[li]High School: its own program and hangout for teens.
I'm listed here under the Co-op. It's full, and that's great!
But I need my traffic going to Group 1, the community, because it has unlimited seats.
How do I make sure my growth, rankings, and ads point to the right group?
Can I switch which group I'm listed under? Or is there a better way to set this up? Thanks so much!

## Comments

- **Mark Novikov** (, 3 likes): You're more than welcome to run multiple groups, but for the purposes of the platinum competition \(hitting MRR milestones / prizes\), only 1 group is counted, so our recommendation is to go all-in on one.
  
  Regardless of this though, all of your groups are eligible to have ads run to them as long as "growth boost" is on in your discovery settings.
