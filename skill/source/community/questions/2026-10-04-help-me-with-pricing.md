# Help me with pricing!

Hey, everyone! @Member e627 and I are excited to be here, and we're about to start promoting our community, Ranked & Recommended \(AI SEO\).

Our plan is to launch at $77/mo for the first 100 members, then go up to $97/mo and slowly increase over time.

I just finished the hour-long interview with Nick Saraev, and I noticed he said he wished he didn't increase his prices so high because he feels he priced out a large chunk of his audience, but he can't dial it back.

I also watched the interview with Samuel G and he mentioned his community is only $25/mo, which is crazy to me. Truly a no-brainer deal.

So I'm torn. I want to make sure we're making enough money per person that it's sustainable, but also I want an incredible no-brainer deal. I think $77/mo is an amazing deal, but it's definitely not the same no-brainer that Samuel's is. That said, I am in another AI SEO community that is $299/mo with over 500 members and we offer just as much value as he does, plus it's packaged in an easier-to-understand way which makes it much more valuable IMO.

Perhaps I'm overthinking it. What do you guys think? Just go with the original $77/mo and move from there? Go lower? Higher?
