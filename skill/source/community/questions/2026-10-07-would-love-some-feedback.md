# Would love some feedback

Would love some thoughts on how you’d structure this.

Quick context: I previously built and sold an agency after acquiring 500+ clients. Since then I’ve mainly done fractional consulting + paid workshops/products, but I’m now building a much more scalable info-product/community model.
I’m thinking:
Free \(currently 212 members from one launch post\) -> -$47/mo Pro -> $1,250/mo Program \(AOR\) -> higher-level DFY implementation / fractional COO

I watched the mindset video around people paying for access to you rather than content, which made me question whether I’m overthinking the content differentiation between Pro and AOR.

AOR is much more personalised, diagnostic, custom roadmap, direct access/support and help applying everything specifically to their agency.

I currently have two communities so my concern is whether having two paid levels in one community changes things. I don’t want someone thinking “I can get all the IP for $47, so why would I pay $1,250?”

Would you put Free + Pro + AOR all inside one Skool and differentiate mainly through access/support/implementation?

And would you keep the free tier at all, or go straight into paid?

Long term I want products + recurring membership to drive most of the revenue, with AOR as the premium backend.
