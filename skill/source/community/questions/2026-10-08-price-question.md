# Price question

I’m wondering what the best way to handle this would be.
Should I announce on my board that the rules will be changing and give Tier 1 members the option to cancel their subscriptions or upgrade to Tier 2?
Or would I need to automatically move existing Tier 1 members to Tier 2?
Since weekly meetings are not the only difference between the tiers, I don’t think automatically moving members from Tier 1 to Tier 2 would be the right approach.
What would you recommend?
