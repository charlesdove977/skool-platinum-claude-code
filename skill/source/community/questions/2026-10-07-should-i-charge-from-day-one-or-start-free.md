# Should I Charge From Day One or Start Free?

I’m currently building a new Skool community called RAS AI Academy for Farsi-speaking entrepreneurs, developers, and people who want to learn how to use AI to build real products and businesses.
The community will include courses like building production-ready products with AI, AI for business, live calls, project feedback, code and architecture reviews, and practical resources.
Since I’m just getting started on Skool, I don’t have a lot of Classroom content uploaded yet.
For someone at this stage, would you recommend charging for access from the beginning?
Or is it generally better to start with a free community, build up the Classroom content and engagement first, and then introduce a paid membership later?

## Comments

- **Mark Novikov** (, 2 likes): I would recommend charging from the beginning because, after all, they're getting access to you from day one, and that's worth $.
