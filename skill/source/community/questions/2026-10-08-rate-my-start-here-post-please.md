# Rate my Start Here Post Please

I’m glad you’re here!

The goal of CYBER+ is simple: help you
get into Cybersecurity by building the skills, hands-on experience and evidence you need to become job-ready for your first blue-team role.

But before you jump into the training, introduce yourself below!

Share:
👋 Your name
📍 Where you’re from
💻 What you currently do for work
🎯 The cybersecurity role you’re aiming for \(SOC Analyst, Cybersecurity Analyst, etc.\)

🚧 The #1 thing you feel is stopping you from landing your first cyber role
