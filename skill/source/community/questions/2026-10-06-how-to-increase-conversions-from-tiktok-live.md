# How to increase conversions from TikTok LIVE?

Hi everyone !
I just did my first proper TikTok LIVE while streaming on Instagram at the same time, and the difference in reach was huge
Instagram:
102 total viewers
52 peak concurrent viewers
Usually around 30 watching at the same time

TikTok:
5,300+ unique viewers
218 peak concurrent viewers
89 average concurrent viewers

However, I only made 2 sales at €29/month from the live, so I feel like there is a lot of room to improve the conversion
I sell a pastry training membership
During the LIVE, I make a pastry from start to finish and try to give as much value as possible : techniques, mistakes to avoid, explanations, etc.

Throughout the live, I make several CTAs explaining that inside the membership we go much deeper, with 100+ videos, 150+ recipes, weekly live coaching and monthly masterclasses

What I'm wondering is :
Is this the right approach for selling through LIVE ?
Should I keep making several short CTAs throughout the stream ?
Or would it be better to focus entirely on the pastry/tutorial, build desire for 1–2 hours, and then do one proper sales pitch at the end ?

For those of you who generate consistent sales from TikTok LIVE, what does your live structure look like ? How often do you pitch, and what has worked best for you ?
