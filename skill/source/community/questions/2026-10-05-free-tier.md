# Free tier?

I have gotten some mixed answers on this one. My community currently has a free tier, then a premium \($20/mo\), then a VIP \($250/mo\). Is it a bad idea to have a free tier? Or is it a good way to get people in the door? I have hosted my community on other platforms before, but this is my first time using Skool. I’m open to suggestions!

My community is for busy, Christian moms who want to get fit/lose weight and improve their family life. The free tier basically just gets access to the community feed but doesn’t get the weekly workout videos, monthly challenges, livestreams & webinars, etc.

## Comments
