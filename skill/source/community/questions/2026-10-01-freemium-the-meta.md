# Freemium The META? 👀

Hey all, happy to be here amongst all you talented Skoolers 💎

So I've just launched and have around 300k followers on IG + around 30k Youtube subs.

Launch has given me some interesting data...

We had a waitlist, and I have a Manychat automation popping off as we speak.

However when leads get to the landing page the conversions seem to be low... Perhaps there's still a bit of friction when met with the price tag?

I have training programmes & nutrition guidance within my community as the core offer, and we have a 90 day challenge going on. I think the 90 day challenge is what's really attracting people, but again there seems to be friction when they get to the door.

After doing some research on Skool, and watching some Synthesizer stuff... Freemium seems to be the META. I plan on having the video modules & nutrition stuff be available, but training programmes are locked behind the tiers.

I also see a bit of a Freemium + Low Ticket VS Freemium + High Ticket thing going on.

I was personally thinking of having all three available \(Free Tier, Low Ticket Tier, High Ticket Tier\).

Any thoughts or advice?
