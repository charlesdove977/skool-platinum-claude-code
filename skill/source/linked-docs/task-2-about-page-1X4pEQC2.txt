﻿Skool VSL Framework
Copy this structure for a high-converting Skool about page video
1. Introduction
Promise
Tell them what you’re going to teach them. Make it interesting enough for them to buy-in and watch the full video.
Example: "In this video, I'll walk you through how to lose 20 lbs without sacrificing the foods you love."
Pain
Call out their current frustrations / problems 
Example: "If you've tried 20 different diets, hate going to pool parties, see everyone around you losing weight but you — I'll be able to help you."
Proof
"I know I can help you because I helped take [Person X] from [Before] to [After], [Person A] from [Before] to [After], and [Person C] from [Before] to [After]..."
2. Body
Explain how what's inside your community will help them achieve their outcome.
* Weekly Q&A Calls – get personalized support, ask me anything
* Private Community Access – be held accountable
* 15+ training videos – day-by-day roadmap to lose 10 lbs
3. Objections
Address the top 3-5 concerns:
Format: What they think → Why it's wrong → What's actually right → Proof
4. Closing CTA
Create Urgency/Scarcity
* "The price is likely to go up soon"
* Limited spots available
* Bonus that expires
* Early member benefits
Direct Call to Action
"I'll see you inside the group”
