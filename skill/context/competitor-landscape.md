## How to fill this
1. Search Skool Discovery for your niche keywords and note the top 10 to 20 groups by members.
2. For each, read the public about page: price, members, one-line positioning, deliverables, guarantee, cover style, about video yes/no.
3. Pick the 3 to 5 paid groups closest to your offer as benchmarks.
4. Write the patterns the winners share and the gaps none of them cover.
