## Numbers worth knowing by heart
- Platinum milestones (recurring MRR growth): $1k / $5k / $10k / $20k / $30k at days 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 150; +$1K MRR checkpoint by Nov 30. Only recurring revenue counts (chunk 09).
- Tech niche price band: $70 to $110/month; money niche $65 to $200 (chunk 02).
- $100k/month = 500 members at $200, 1,000 at $100, 2,000 at $50 (chunk 02).
- Annual discount band: 20 to 40 percent off 12 × monthly (chunk 02).
- About-page conversion: $100k/month groups average about 1.3% per the Skool team; cold-traffic range cited 1 to 5 percent (chunk 10).
- Hobby vs Pro plan break-even: about $1,300 MRR (chunks 02, 09).
- Mentors (self-reported): Nick ~$3M/yr solo at $184/mo; Jade $155k MRR, 77% retention; Josh ~$140k MRR, LTV $800+; James Lu $99/mo, 88% retention; Samuel G $25/mo, ~80% of traffic via affiliates (chunk 08).
